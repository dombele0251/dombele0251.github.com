I create professional photography for brands, personal trainers and businesses who want to stand out online. Strong visuals help communicate your brand, attract clients and elevate your presence across websites and social media.

The Process

Before every shoot I like to sit down with my clients and understand the objective of the project. Whether it's content for social media, advertising or personal branding, the goal is to create images that represent the brand clearly.

Together we create a moodboard that defines the visual direction of the shoot. This helps align references, lighting style, mood and overall aesthetic before the shoot takes place.

We then outline a simple shot list including poses, compositions and key visuals we want to capture. This gives structure to the shoot while still leaving space for creativity.

By the time we begin shooting, everyone has a clear idea of the objective and the final deliverables we want to achieve.
