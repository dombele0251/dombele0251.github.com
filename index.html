I create professional photography for brands, personal trainers and businesses who want to stand out online. Strong visuals help communicate your brand, attract clients and elevate your presence across websites and social media.

The Process

Before every shoot I like to sit down with my clients and understand the objective of the project.

Together we create a moodboard that defines the visual direction of the shoot.

We then outline a simple shot list including poses, compositions and key visuals we want to capture.

By the time we begin shooting, everyone has a clear idea of the objective and the final deliverables.
