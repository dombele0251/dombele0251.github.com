The person behind the images

A considered eye.
A crafted image.

A background in professional photo retouching informs a precise approach to light, tone and image-making.

After years spent working behind the scenes, I was drawn to step into the process itself — to be closer to the moment and create the images, not just refine them.

Based in the South West and working in London, Bristol, Taunton and across the UK, I’m available for editorial and commercial commissions.
