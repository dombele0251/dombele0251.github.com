ABOUT

Dombele is a UK-based photographer working across fashion, editorial, and live music. The work grew out of a background in photo editing and retouching — years spent inside other people's images before picking up the camera to make his own.

That eye for post-production informs everything on set: an instinct for light, tone, and what will hold in the edit. The shooting process is where it all comes together.

Available for commissions across the UK.
