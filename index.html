About the Studio

A background in professional photo retouching informs my precise approach to light, tone, and image-making.

After years spent working behind the scenes, I was drawn to step into the process itself — to be closer to the moment and create the images, not just refine them.

Based between London, Bristol, and Taunton, I am available for editorial and commercial commissions across the UK.
