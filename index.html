More than a photoshoot

Come for the portraits. Stay for the people.

The Exeter Headshot Lounge brings a professional pop-up portrait studio together with a relaxed social networking space. Get a fresh, professionally photographed headshot, meet local creatives and professionals, enjoy drinks and a curated music atmosphere, and make new connections.

Whether you’re a student, founder, creative, performer, model or working professional, the lounge is designed to make meeting people — and getting a great portrait — feel natural.

Who’s behind it

Hosted by Dombele Studio

The Headshot Lounge is presented and hosted by Dombele Studio, led by founder and photographer Dombele Chindandy.

Dombele’s photography work includes projects with UK artists and brands such as Tommy Richman, Odeal and Jay1, alongside fashion influencers, across more than 40 UK shoots. The event brings that professional photography experience into an accessible, relaxed pop-up setting.

The Studio

Dombelestudio is a photography and creative production studio helping independent creators, brands and businesses elevate their identity through considered, high-end imagery. Working across commercial, editorial and creative projects, the studio brings together photography, creative direction and shoot production.

With experience working across a wide range of environments, Dombele brings a considered, adaptable approach to every shoot. A strong understanding of light, reflections and photographic equipment allows him to respond to different conditions, make the most of each setting and overcome the challenges that come with it. Whether working in a controlled studio or on location, the focus remains on creating the best possible images, whatever the circumstances.
